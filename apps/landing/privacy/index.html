Privacy

Hillpath is a prototype. This page says what it does today, not what we hope to do.

Records stay on the device

Activities, reminders, answers and results are stored on the phone or tablet, sealed with a key that the browser keeps and does not let apps read out. There are no accounts and no analytics. This prototype has no server that receives your records.

Sharing without internet

Devices in a family circle share by showing a moving code on one screen and scanning it with the other. Each share is encrypted with a key that only the circle holds and signed by the sending device. Removing a device changes the key, so it cannot read anything new.

You choose who sees what

The family view has a Privacy screen with a switch for each kind of sharing: family circle, a community health worker, a report for a doctor, and an encrypted backup. Each choice records how agreement was given. Sharing outside the family is blocked when family agreed on the person's behalf without a checked guardian.

Medicine and safety

Hillpath never gives dose advice. If a dose is not confirmed, the person is told not to take another, and the family is asked to check.

Law and review

The design follows the ideas of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: notice, separate consent for each purpose, easy withdrawal, and a lawful guardian for a person who cannot decide. It has not had a legal review. Do not use it with real patient records until it has.
